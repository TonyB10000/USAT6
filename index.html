High-Resolution Year-by-Year Pressure: The Siphoned & The Squeezed (T-6 to T-3)

The Kinematic Formula

Instead of looking at gross wealth, we measure the structural "squeeze" on a demographic band by dividing its population footprint by its actual wealth share.

// P = F / A

P_Band = Pop% / Wealth%

Pop% = Fixed Band Demo %

Wealth% = [y2 - y1]

The Measured Bands

  • T-3: Upper Middle (15%)
  • T-4: Squeezed Middle (30%)
  • T-5: Working Class (30%)
  • T-6: The Base (20%)

The Apex (T-0 and T-1) and their direct buffers (T-2) operate on entirely different kinematic rules and are omitted here.
