The Kinematic Formula

Instead of looking at gross wealth, we measure the structural "squeeze" on a demographic band by dividing its population footprint by its actual wealth share.

// P = F / A

P_T6 = Pop% / Wealth%

Pop% = 20 (Fixed)

Wealth% = [y2 - y1]
